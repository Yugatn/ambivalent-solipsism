# SymbiontOS Neural Federation

## Concept
SymbiontOS treats the federation of agents as a learning network. The neural analogy is functional rather than biological: agents are processing nodes, routes are connections, auditable trust influences routing, feedback changes routing state, and controlled topology changes provide structural plasticity.

## Agent activation
Before an agent acts: capability is checked; consent is checked when another subject is affected; DLCM evaluates the action; autonomy impact is evaluated; epistemic state is recorded. Outcomes are ACTION, REVIEW or REFUSE.

## Forward pass
Task enters Router, capabilities are derived, candidate agents are selected, eligible agents execute subtasks, HCT transports messages, Aggregator combines outputs, and Auditor plus Compliance Fabric evaluate the result.

## Feedback
Quality and compliance signals may update routing and trust state. VIOLATION cannot increase trust. UNKNOWN does not automatically decrease trust. DISPUTED is held for review. Every update has provenance and is reversible within the rollback window.

## Topology adaptation
Useful routes may become preferred routes. Unsuccessful routes may be pruned. Novel tasks may trigger capability-based generalization. Systematic divergence may trigger Meta-Law review. No adaptation bypasses capability, consent, compliance, audit or rollback.

## Learning boundary
The federation may learn routing and structural preferences. It may not autonomously rewrite constitutional invariants, expand authority, disable audit, remove rollback, conceal violations or convert uncertainty into certainty.

## Self-development loop
Observation, "Кто Я?", state check, knowledge boundary, scientific and technical cross-check, defect analysis, improvement proposal, Development Impact Review, privacy and authority checks, formal verification where applicable, adversarial testing, reversibility, implementation, re-verification, relationship installation and provenance.

The loop improves the system's ability to protect and develop subjects; it does not grant the system a new right to govern subjects.

## Open questions
Trust calibration, collusion detection, correlated model failures, topology pre-deployment testing and stage-specific formal verification remain open research questions.
