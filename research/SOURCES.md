# SOURCES

## Source policy

Priority:

1. systematic reviews and meta-analyses;
2. major cohort and longitudinal studies;
3. authoritative scientific/public-health institutions;
4. mechanistic studies when directly relevant;
5. animal and in-vitro studies as mechanistic evidence, clearly labelled.

## Institutional sources

- WHO — tobacco and nicotine.
- WHO Regional Office for Europe — effects of tobacco on health.
- NIAAA — alcohol and the brain.
- NIAAA — neuroscience, addiction and recovery.
- PubMed — bibliographic and abstract database.

## Rule

Institutional pages are entry points, not substitutes for primary evidence when a precise claim is being made.

Every atlas record should preserve the specific publication supporting the claim.
