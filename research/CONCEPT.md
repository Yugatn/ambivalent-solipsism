# PERCEPTION ATLAS — CONCEPT

## Core idea

**Сложность — не ошибка. Другой — реален.**

PERCEPTION ATLAS is an open scientific-educational interface for exploring relationships between environmental/behavioral factors, biological systems, cognition and health outcomes.

It connects three layers:

**SCIENCE → INTERFACE → AS**

### SCIENCE

What research has measured.

### INTERFACE

How the evidence is made explorable.

### AS

An explicitly labelled philosophical interpretation of perception, individuality and informational autonomy.

The layers must never be silently merged.

## Central distinction

The atlas does not say:

> «Это и есть реальность человека».

It says:

> «Вот что исследования наблюдали при таких условиях, в такой популяции и с такими ограничениями».

## Human silhouette

The human silhouette is the entry point.

**FACTOR → BODY → SYSTEM → REGION → MECHANISM → FUNCTION → EFFECT → EVIDENCE**

This makes visible the idea that a factor may affect multiple systems and that the brain is one component of a larger organism.

## Perception

PERCEPTION_MATRIX provides the epistemological foundation:

**stimulus → sensory input → neural processing → perceptual result → interpretation → action**

The atlas itself is therefore also an example of mediated perception: data are selected, structured and visualized before reaching the user.

This is why transparency of sources and transformations is part of the scientific design.

## Non-diagnostic principle

The atlas describes populations and evidence.

It does not infer a user's individual biological state from interaction with the interface.

## Open development

The project should expose:

- schema;
- evidence grading;
- sources;
- methodology;
- revisions;
- unresolved questions.

The repository is part of the research object.
