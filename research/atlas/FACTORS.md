# PERCEPTION ATLAS — Factors

## 1. Alcohol
Track separately:

- ethanol exposure;
- acute exposure;
- chronic exposure;
- drinking pattern;
- alcohol use disorder.

An alcohol claim must specify which exposure is being studied.

## 2. Smoking and nicotine
Do not collapse:

- nicotine;
- tobacco smoking;
- tobacco smoke;
- combustion products;
- smoking behavior;
- electronic-cigarette aerosol.

These are related but scientifically different exposures.

## 3. Sleep
Important dimensions:

- duration;
- regularity;
- fragmentation;
- circadian alignment;
- sleep quality;
- acute deprivation versus chronic restriction.

## 4. Stress
Separate:

- acute stress;
- chronic stress;
- perceived stress;
- physiological stress responses;
- social context.

A subjective stress report and a measured physiological marker are not identical variables.

## 5. Nutrition
Atlas should prefer dietary patterns and measurable nutrient exposures over simplistic «good food / bad food» categories.

Relevant dimensions include:

- energy balance;
- protein and micronutrient adequacy;
- dietary pattern;
- metabolic context;
- duration.

## 6. Physical activity
Separate:

- acute exercise;
- regular aerobic activity;
- resistance training;
- sedentary behavior;
- training load;
- recovery.

## 7. Pollution
Potential exposures include:

- particulate matter;
- nitrogen dioxide;
- ozone;
- heavy metals;
- occupational pollutants.

Route and duration of exposure matter.

## 8. Other
Future layers may include:

- medications;
- infections;
- recreational drugs;
- occupational exposures;
- environmental factors;
- social environment.

Each new category must receive its own evidence definition.
