# PERCEPTION ATLAS — Mechanisms

Mechanism is the bridge between exposure and observed outcome.

## Core mechanism classes

### Neurotransmission
Changes in signaling between neurons or in receptor systems.

### Vascular effects
Changes in blood flow, vascular tone or vascular integrity.

### Inflammation
Immune signaling and inflammatory processes. The term must not be used as a universal explanation.

### Oxidative stress
An imbalance involving reactive species and antioxidant defenses. It is a biological process, not synonymous with irreversible tissue damage.

### Excitotoxicity
Pathological overactivation of excitatory signaling under specific conditions.

### Endocrine signaling
Hormonal changes and altered feedback regulation.

### Metabolic effects
Changes in energy use, substrate availability or metabolic regulation.

### Neuroadaptation
Longer-term changes in neural systems following repeated exposure.

### DNA / cellular damage
Specific molecular or cellular endpoints must be named. «DNA damage» is not a sufficient description without specifying the measured marker and tissue.

### Dysbiosis
Changes in microbial communities. Association with a health outcome does not by itself establish that microbiome change is the cause.

## Mechanism confidence

Each mechanism should be marked:

- established;
- supported;
- proposed;
- unknown.

A plausible mechanism is not automatically an established mechanism.
