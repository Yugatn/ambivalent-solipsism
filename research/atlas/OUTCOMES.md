# PERCEPTION ATLAS — Outcomes

Outcomes are the final observable or clinically meaningful layer.

## Cognitive

- attention;
- memory;
- learning;
- working memory;
- processing speed;
- cognitive flexibility;
- executive control;
- decision making.

## Emotional and affective

- emotional regulation;
- salience processing;
- stress response;
- reward processing;
- interoception.

## Motor

- coordination;
- reaction time;
- balance;
- motor learning.

## Physiological

- blood pressure;
- respiratory function;
- metabolic markers;
- hormonal measures;
- immune markers.

## Structural

Examples include:

- regional volume;
- tissue integrity;
- vascular measures;
- imaging-derived network properties.

Structural findings must not automatically be translated into subjective or behavioral impairment.

## Risk outcomes

Risk is population-level unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Preferred language:

> «associated with increased risk of...»

rather than:

> «will cause...»

## Reversibility

Reversibility is represented as an evidence question, not a binary assumption.

Possible statuses:

- demonstrated recovery;
- partial recovery;
- uncertain;
- persistent association;
- insufficient evidence.

Time scale must be specified whenever possible.
