# Stress — Atlas Node

## Definition
Stress is not a single biological variable.

Atlas distinguishes:

- acute stress;
- chronic stress;
- perceived stress;
- physiological stress responses;
- social context;
- controllability and predictability.

## Mechanisms
Potential pathways include autonomic regulation, endocrine signaling, immune signaling and changes in attention or emotional processing.

The exact pathway depends on the type and duration of stress.

## Subjective and biological levels
A person's reported stress and a physiological marker are related but not identical measurements.

The subjective experience remains a valid layer of the Atlas rather than an error to be discarded.

## Unknown
Long-term outcomes are heterogeneous and strongly context-dependent.
