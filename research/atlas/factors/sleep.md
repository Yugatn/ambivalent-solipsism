# Sleep — Atlas Node

## Definition
Sleep is represented as a multidimensional exposure/state rather than a single number.

## Dimensions
- duration;
- regularity;
- fragmentation;
- circadian timing;
- sleep quality;
- acute deprivation;
- chronic restriction.

## Interpretation
A short sleep episode and long-term chronic sleep restriction are different exposures.

Sleep should also be represented as an interacting variable: stress, alcohol, nicotine, physical activity and environment may alter sleep, while sleep may in turn modify cognition and emotional regulation.

## Cognitive layer
Atlas tracks attention, working memory, learning, executive control and processing speed separately.

A change in one task does not establish a global change in intelligence.

## Unknown
Individual sleep need, chronotype and context vary. The Atlas therefore avoids a universal «optimal number» as a substitute for evidence.
