# Alcohol — Atlas Node

## Definition
Alcohol in this Atlas means exposure to ethanol. Beverage type is not treated as the biological exposure itself.

## Exposure dimensions
- dose;
- frequency;
- pattern;
- duration;
- acute versus chronic exposure;
- presence or absence of alcohol use disorder.

## Brain
NIAAA reports that alcohol interferes with brain communication pathways and can affect brain structure and function. Long-term heavy drinking can alter neurons and brain systems involved in memory, decision-making, impulse control, attention and sleep regulation. citeturn0search6turn0search11

## Plasticity and recovery
The brain is plastic. NIAAA describes plasticity as relevant both to addiction and recovery; recovery can involve neurological and psychological improvement. This does not justify a universal claim of complete reversal. citeturn0search11

## Cancer layer
WHO states that no safe level of alcohol consumption can be established for cancer risk and that risk increases with consumption. citeturn0search0turn0search1
