# Physical Activity — Atlas Node

## Dimensions
- aerobic activity;
- resistance training;
- acute exercise;
- regular activity;
- sedentary behavior;
- training load;
- recovery.

## Principle
«Physical activity» is not one exposure. Dose, intensity, frequency, baseline fitness and recovery matter.

## Brain and cognition
The Atlas will distinguish acute cognitive effects from longer-term associations with health and cognition.

## System interaction
Physical activity can connect simultaneously to:

```
cardiovascular
respiratory
metabolic
musculoskeletal
nervous
```

The Atlas therefore avoids a single universal «exercise effect».

## Unknown
Effects can differ according to age, baseline health, training status and type of activity.
