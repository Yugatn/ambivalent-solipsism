# Material State History Schema

## Object record
- material_identity
- initial_state
- temperature
- pressure
- gravitational_context
- acceleration_environment
- radiation_exposure
- chemical_environment
- stress
- microstructure
- processing_history
- measured_properties
- uncertainty

## Principle
An object's identity label does not imply invariant properties. Properties are state- and history-dependent.

## Space application
Microgravity experiments should record altered transport mechanisms and boundary conditions rather than describing matter as obeying different physical laws.
