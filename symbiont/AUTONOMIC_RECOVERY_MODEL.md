# Autonomic Recovery Model

## Purpose
Model dynamic response and recovery rather than treating one autonomic measurement as a stable health score.

## Phases
- Baseline
- Trigger
- Response
- Peak
- Recovery
- Return or adaptation

## Measures
Potential inputs include HR, HRV, respiration, blood pressure, subjective arousal and recovery time.

## Interpretation
Recovery trajectory is more informative than an isolated value when the measurement protocol is reliable.
HRV must not be treated as a universal stress, vagal or health score.

## Confounders
Sleep, activity, posture, breathing, caffeine, medication, illness, time of day and measurement conditions can affect autonomic data.

## Safety
Unexpected deterioration or clinically concerning symptoms must trigger the Medical Safety Gate rather than automated optimization.

## Principle
**Recovery Before Optimization.**
