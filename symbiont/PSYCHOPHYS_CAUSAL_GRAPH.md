# Psychophysiological Causal Graph

## Purpose
Prevent simple one-cause explanations of complex mind-body observations.

## Core domains
- Context
- Appraisal
- Subjective state
- CNS state
- Autonomic regulation
- Endocrine regulation
- Immune regulation
- Behavior
- Sleep
- Nutrition
- Social environment
- Medication
- Disease processes
- Measurement

## Causal discipline
A temporal sequence is not sufficient to establish causality.
Every causal candidate should identify alternative explanations, confounders, mediators and measurement artifacts.

## Multi-hypothesis rule
SymbiontOS should retain competing causal hypotheses when evidence does not discriminate between them.

## AS integration
**Correlation ≠ Causation.**
**Observation ≠ Mechanism.**
**Model ≠ Subject.**
