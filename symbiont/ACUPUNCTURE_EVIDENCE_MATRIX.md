# Acupuncture Evidence Matrix

## Required dimensions
- exact technique;
- condition;
- population;
- comparator;
- outcome;
- follow-up;
- effect size;
- certainty;
- risk of bias;
- sham definition;
- adherence;
- adverse events;
- applicability;
- replication.

## Interpretation
Do not transfer evidence from one acupuncture technique, condition or outcome to another without justification.
Do not treat a difference from sham as proof of the traditional mechanism.
Do not treat subjective symptom change as proof of structural cure.
