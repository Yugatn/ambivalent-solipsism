# Spaceflight State Protocol

## Timepoints
- Preflight baseline
- Launch/early adaptation
- Inflight steady state
- Long-duration adaptation
- Re-entry
- Early postflight
- Recovery

## Domains
- gravitational context;
- vestibular state;
- proprioception;
- body fluid distribution;
- cardiovascular state;
- musculoskeletal state;
- sleep/circadian state;
- cognitive performance;
- affect;
- subjective body schema;
- autonomic state;
- environmental exposures;
- workload;
- social context.

## Rule
Each observation retains time, method, uncertainty and exposure context.

## AS invariant
**State trajectory ≠ Subject identity.**
