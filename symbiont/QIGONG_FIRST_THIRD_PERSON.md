# Qigong First-Person and Third-Person Data

## First-person
Track perceived calmness, tension, warmth, fatigue, concentration, bodily sensations, pain, breath comfort and perceived energy.

## Third-person
Where appropriate, track HR, HRV, respiration, blood pressure, movement, sleep and validated functional outcomes.

## Separation
An experience of qi is recorded as subjective data unless a specific validated physiological correlate has been established.

**Subjective Energy ≠ Physical Energy by Default.**
**Traditional Concept ≠ Anatomical Structure.**
