# Qigong Evidence Matrix

## Required dimensions
- Specific practice
- Population
- Condition or goal
- Comparator
- Outcome
- Study design
- Effect estimate
- Certainty
- Risk of bias
- Follow-up
- Adherence
- Adverse events
- Replication
- Applicability

## Interpretation
Do not use evidence for one qigong protocol to validate every qigong practice.
Do not generalize an outcome-specific effect to global health.

## Evidence labels
OBSERVED, SUBJECTIVE, PHYSIOLOGICAL, CORRELATED, CAUSAL-CANDIDATE, CLINICALLY-VALIDATED, NOT_TESTED, CONFLICTED.
