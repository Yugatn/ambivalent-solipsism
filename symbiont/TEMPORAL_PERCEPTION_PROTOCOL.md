# Temporal Perception Protocol

## Required time fields
- physical_reference_time
- clock_time
- event_time
- biological_state
- circadian_phase
- subjective_duration
- retrospective_duration
- prospective_duration
- autobiographical_position
- social_schedule
- environmental_context
- uncertainty

## Developmental extension
Record prenatal, neonatal, infant, child, adolescent and adult timing evidence separately. Do not infer adult autobiographical time from prenatal sensory responsiveness.

## AS invariant
**Clock time, biological time and subjective time are related but non-identical variables.**
