# Subjective–Physiological Discordance

## Purpose
Treat disagreement between first-person and third-person data as a meaningful observation rather than automatic failure.

## Patterns
- Subjective improvement with physiological worsening.
- Subjective worsening with physiological stability.
- Physiological change without reported subjective change.
- Behavioral change without corresponding measured physiological change.
- Subjective and physiological improvement together.
- Subjective and physiological deterioration together.

## Interpretation
Discordance requires context, measurement-quality review and alternative hypotheses.
It must not be used alone for diagnosis.

## Workflow
Record both data streams; verify timing; verify signal quality; review context; compare baseline; generate competing hypotheses; apply Safety Gate when risk is present.

## AS principle
**First-person evidence and third-person evidence can disagree without either automatically being false.**
