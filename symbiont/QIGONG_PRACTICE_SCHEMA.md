# Qigong Practice Schema

## Purpose
Describe a qigong intervention precisely enough for comparison and reproducibility.

## Fields
- practice_name
- lineage_or_style
- movement_components
- posture
- breathing_pattern
- attentional_focus
- imagery_or_intention
- duration
- frequency
- intensity
- instructor_or_self_guided
- individual_or_group
- adherence
- subjective_response
- physiological_response
- functional_outcome
- adverse_events
- evidence_status

## Rule
Do not collapse different qigong styles into one intervention when their components materially differ.
