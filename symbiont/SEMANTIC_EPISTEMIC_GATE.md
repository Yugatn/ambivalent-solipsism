# Semantic Reality Epistemic Gate

## Before converting meaning into a reality claim
1. Identify the underlying observation.
2. Identify the Subject and context.
3. Separate semantic interpretation from physical description.
4. Record alternative interpretations.
5. Preserve uncertainty and residual.
6. Check whether the claim is individual, social or population-level.
7. Test whether the claim makes a falsifiable prediction.

## Prohibited inference
Do not infer that because perception is semantically organized, external reality is created by the observer.

## Rule
**Semantic mediation is an epistemic fact about cognition, not proof of semantic ontology of the universe.**
