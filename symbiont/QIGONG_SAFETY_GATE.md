# Qigong Safety Gate

## Checks
- Current symptoms
- Relevant medical conditions
- Falls risk
- Cardiopulmonary symptoms
- Pain
- Dizziness
- Fatigue
- Practice intensity
- Medication context
- Ability to stop safely

## Hard stop
Qigong must not be used to delay urgent medical evaluation when red flags are present.

## Principle
**Complementary Practice ≠ Replacement for Necessary Medical Care.**
**Right to Stop is part of Subject autonomy.**
