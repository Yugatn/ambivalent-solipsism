# Quantum Physics and Psychology Synthesis

## Epistemic separation
Quantum physics is a physical theory. Quantum cognition is a formal modeling framework using quantum probability structures for cognitive phenomena. Quantum consciousness refers to hypotheses about physical quantum mechanisms in consciousness.

**Quantum cognition ≠ quantum brain.**
**Quantum-like model ≠ quantum physical mechanism.**
**Quantum observer ≠ conscious Subject.**
**Quantum superposition ≠ Social Superposition.**
**Quantum entanglement ≠ social relationship.**
**Quantum uncertainty ≠ metaphysical solipsism.**

## Useful context for AS
Quantum cognition is useful when contextuality, order effects, interference-like probability effects or state-dependent judgments provide an empirically testable modeling problem.
Classical probability remains a serious alternative. A quantum-like model should therefore add explanatory or predictive value rather than receive authority from quantum terminology alone.

## Measurement interaction
Psychological measurement can depend on question order, prior judgments, context and state.
Working AS principle:
**Observation is a function of Subject State, Context, Measurement Procedure, History and Uncertainty.**

Therefore a profile is dynamic:
**Profile at time t and context c is not necessarily a fixed identity.**

## Complementarity
Neural, psychological, motivational, social and phenomenological descriptions may capture different aspects of a subject.
Complementary descriptions do not mean every contradictory proposition is true.

## Uncertainty taxonomy
Distinguish physical uncertainty, statistical uncertainty, epistemic uncertainty, model uncertainty and psychological ambiguity.
Unknown is not automatically quantum.

## Quantum consciousness boundary
Quantum theories of consciousness remain hypotheses requiring physical feasibility, mechanistic specification and empirical testability.
AS may preserve metaphysical claims about soul or rebirth as philosophical hypotheses, but quantum physics must not be used as proof of them.

## Anti-Quantum-Washing test
Before using a quantum term ask:
1. Is there a physical quantum mechanism?
2. Is the phenomenon empirical?
3. Is a quantum-like formal model actually required?
4. What classical alternatives exist?
5. What prediction distinguishes the models?
6. Has the result replicated?

## Integration
The quantum layer extends AS epistemology with contextuality, measurement interaction, state dependence and disciplined uncertainty.
It does not alter the core ontology:
**Model ≠ Subject.**
**Reality = Model + Residual/UNKNOWN.**
**Subject ≠ Measurement.**
