# Spaceflight Consciousness Epistemic Gate

## Before claiming consciousness change
1. Specify whether the observation is perceptual, vestibular, cognitive, affective, sleep-related, behavioral or phenomenological.
2. Separate microgravity from isolation, radiation, CO2, workload and circadian effects.
3. Identify objective measures and first-person reports separately.
4. Test alternative explanations.
5. Distinguish transient adaptation from persistent change.

## Prohibited inference
Do not infer that microgravity proves expanded consciousness, altered soul, metaphysical freedom from the body or any other metaphysical conclusion.

## Rule
**Altered experience is evidence of altered experience; it is not by itself evidence of a new ontology of consciousness.**
