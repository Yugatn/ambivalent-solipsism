# Qigong Session Protocol

## Session sequence
1. Baseline.
2. Record exact practice.
3. Record intention and context.
4. Perform practice.
5. Record immediate subjective response.
6. Record physiological measures when available.
7. Observe recovery.
8. Record delayed outcome.
9. Record adverse events.
10. Compare repeated sessions with baseline.

## Data principle
Practice dose and adherence must be recorded before interpreting outcomes.

## Stop rule
Pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, falls, marked deterioration or other concerning symptoms require stopping the practice and applying the Safety Gate.
