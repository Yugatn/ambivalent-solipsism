# Acupuncture Safety Gate

## Pre-session checks
- indication and treatment goal;
- relevant diagnosis or medical assessment;
- anticoagulant/bleeding context when relevant;
- infection risk;
- pregnancy and anatomical considerations when relevant;
- implanted devices for electroacupuncture when relevant;
- fainting history;
- neurological or cardiopulmonary red flags;
- practitioner competence;
- sterile single-use equipment;
- emergency escalation plan.

## Stop and escalate
Stop the intervention and seek appropriate medical assessment for severe chest symptoms, neurological deficit, significant bleeding, severe infection signs, loss of consciousness or other concerning deterioration.

## Rule
**Acupuncture ≠ automatically low risk. Safety is protocol-dependent.**
