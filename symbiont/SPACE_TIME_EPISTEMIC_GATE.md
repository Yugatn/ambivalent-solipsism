# Space-Time Epistemic Gate

## Before claiming that time changed
1. Specify the reference frame.
2. Identify whether the claim concerns clock time, proper time or subjective duration.
3. Separate gravitational and velocity effects from environmental and psychological effects.
4. Identify the measurement method.
5. Check alternative explanations for subjective temporal change.
6. Preserve developmental and prenatal uncertainty.

## Prohibited inference
Do not use relativistic time dilation as evidence for metaphysical claims about consciousness, soul, reincarnation or solipsism.

## Rule
**Physical relativity and subjective temporality can both be real without being the same phenomenon.**
